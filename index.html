Command Centre

Command Centre is a private, single-user assistant that Connor Robinson runs for his own household admin: calendars, travel, car and home maintenance, and personal finances.

It is not a public service. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone other than Connor Robinson.

When Connor signs in, the app reads his Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar with read-only permissions. It cannot send email, change files or edit events.

See the privacy policy for how data is handled.
